## Job Description

Williams and White is a custom contract manufacturer specializing in precision machining and fabrication. We are looking for a **Machine Shop Project Manager** to take ownership of customer jobs from intake through to delivery.

This role sits at the center of operations coordinating between customers, production, vendors to ensure projects are delivered **on time, on budget, and to specification**. If you understand machining, can read drawings, and know how to keep jobs moving in a fast\-paced shop, this will feel like home.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Project \& Job Management**

* Manage projects from Purchase order through to final delivery
* Develop and execute manufacturing plans for machining and fabrication jobs
* Monitor job progress, timelines, and budgets to ensure on\-time delivery

**Production Coordination**

* Work closely with shop floor teams to prioritize and schedule work
* Identify and resolve bottlenecks to keep production moving efficiently
* Ensure drawings, materials, and processes are aligned before release

**Purchasing \& Supply Chain**

* Source and procure materials and subcontract services based on BOMs
* Follow up with vendors to ensure on\-time delivery of components
* Maintain inventory awareness for ongoing and upcoming jobs

**Customer \& Vendor Communication**

* Act as the main point of contact for customers on active projects
* Coordinate with vendors and subcontractors to resolve issues quickly
* Communicate timelines, updates, and challenges clearly and professionally

**Quality \& Documentation**

* Review job packages and ensure all required documentation is complete
* Support quality processes by coordinating inspections and addressing issues
* Track and follow up on NCRs related to assigned projects as needed

**Qualifications \& Experience**

* Minimum **5\+ years experience in machining and/or fabrication environments**
* Experience in **project management within a machine shop or manufacturing setting**
* Strong understanding of machining processes (milling, turning, grinding, etc.)
* Ability to read and interpret **engineering drawings and GD\&T**
* Hands\-on familiarity with **metalworking processes and materials**

**Technical Skills:**

* Experience with ERP systems (JobBOSS or similar preferred)
* BOM and Shop drawing preparation
* SolidWorks or similar CAD experience
* Strong computer skills (MS Office / Google Workspace)
* Comfortable with measuring tools (calipers, micrometers, etc.)

**What We’re Looking For**

* Highly organized and detail\-oriented
* Strong sense of ownership and accountability
* Able to manage multiple jobs in a fast\-paced environment
* Practical, solution\-focused mindset
* Strong communication skills with both shop floor and customers

**Compensation \& Benefits**

* Competitive salary based on experience
* Extended health and dental coverage
* Profit sharing program
* Paid time off
* Casual work environment
