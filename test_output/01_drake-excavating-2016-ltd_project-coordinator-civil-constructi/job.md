## Job Description

**Drake Excavating (2016\) Ltd.** is a civil and general contracting company, based out of the Lower Mainland of BC. We specialize in municipal infrastructure work, including waterworks, roadworks, and utilities projects.

Drake is an exciting, fast\-paced work environment and we are currently seeking a **Project Coordinator** to join our team.

**Key Responsibilities Include:**

**Project Coordination:**

* Attend pre\-construction meetings and liaise with the Project Manager and Contract Administrator in submitting all required contract documents and project related documentation throughout the project
* Prepare, submit and file all contract documents, including obtaining COC / Builder’s Risk Insurance, Bonding, contracts, and WCB Clearance Letters
* Prepare submit and file all submittals and RFI’s throughout the duration of the project
* Submit BC One Calls and renew as needed
* Submit or assist with NOP’s, permits, TMP’s and Notices to Residents
* Manage all business licenses, including renewals
* Prepare close\-out documents for each project, including creating O\&M Manuals, issuing Substantial and Total Completion Requests
* Prepare foreman binders, including printing full\-size drawings on a large format printer
* Liaise with Dispatch in coordinating site facilities at project start\-up, including portable toilets, fencing and container storage
* Liaise with Project Accountant in issuance of Purchase Orders
* Liaise with Project Accountant in booking accommodations for out\-of\-town projects
* Maintain all required project documentation, including truck slips, RFI’s, submittals, meeting minutes, etc., and ensure all are filed in the project folders as needed

**Estimating Administration:**

* Assist and coordinate all aspects of the tender submission process, including preparing and executing bid submission documents
* Add and update the tender board daily, advising estimating team on addendums and mandatory meeting dates
* Download tenders and all addendums as required
* Ensure all online, e\-mail and drop\-off tender submissions are executed on\-time
* Update and maintain key personnel resumes and project reference packages to be included in tender submissions
* Liaise with estimating team to complete technical aspects of all tender submissions
* Request and manage bid bond requests, including obtaining insurance quotes for tender submissions
* Assist with the preparation of Request for Proposals and Pre\-Qualifications, while maintaining efficient communication with estimating team to ensure submissions are technically complete prior to submission

**Qualifications:**

* Bachelor of Civil Engineering (Preferred)
* Experience with project coordination and project administrative tasks
* Strong organizational and time management skills
* Excellent written and verbal English communication skills
* Proficiency with Microsoft Office software, especially Excel
* Familiarity with tender submission processes and documentation
* Detail\-oriented
