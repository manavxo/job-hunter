**Position Overview**

We're growing and looking for a Steel Detailing Project Manager to join our team in Burnaby, BC. In this role, you'll be responsible for overseeing projects from start to finish. This means you'll handle all aspects of project planning, resource allocation, coordination, and reporting.

You'll manage client relationships, project deliverables, and budgets, all while leading your dedicated project team. A proactive approach is essential as you'll be responsible for setting and managing project objectives with both internal teams and external stakeholders, ensuring every project is delivered on time and within budget. You'll also play a key role in developing client proposals, tenders, and consulting reports.
